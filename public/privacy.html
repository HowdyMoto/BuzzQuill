Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

BuzzQuill is a browser-based vector editor. Your drawings are created and kept in your browser and in the files you choose to save them to. BuzzQuill runs no server of its own: we never see, collect, or store your artwork.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts and no analytics. Opening and saving files uses your browser's own file picker, so the only copies of your work are the ones you make.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, BuzzQuill asks Google only for permission to the files you open with it or create with it (the drive.file scope) — never your whole Drive. File contents travel directly between your browser and Google; nothing passes through us, and we keep no record of your Google account. The sign-in token is kept in this tab's session storage and expires after about an hour. Disconnect Google Drive clears it locally and requests revocation from Google. If Google cannot confirm revocation (for example, the token has expired or you are offline), remove BuzzQuill's access from your Google account's security settings. Browser session restore may restore tab storage, so use Disconnect on a shared device.

Local storage

Preferences, your recent-files lists and a snapshot of your open documents (so they come back after a reload) live in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB) and never leave your device.

Questions

The source code is public, and questions or reports are welcome on our issue tracker.
